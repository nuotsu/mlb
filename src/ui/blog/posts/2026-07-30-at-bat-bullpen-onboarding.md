A few game-page upgrades and some first-visit polish. Pitch-by-pitch detail when you're watching a game, workload context in the bullpen, and tips so new visitors know what to do next.

## At-bat sequence

Game pages now have an **At-bat sequence** panel between Plays and Win Probability. Browse each plate appearance with pitch trajectories into the zone, the count and outs, pitch type / speed, and hit metrics when the ball is put in play. Hover a pitch to sync the highlight across the path and the list.

![At-bat sequence of Teoscar Hernández's home run with pitch trajectories and hit metrics](/screenshots/at-bat-sequence.png)

See it on any live or final game, e.g. [Mariners @ Dodgers](/game/823921).

## Bullpen fatigue

The boxscore now includes **Bullpen Fatigue** for each team: recent relief workload as HP bars based on the 14 days before that game, with innings and pitch totals, plus throw side next to each pitcher.

![Bullpen Fatigue gauges for Dodgers relievers](/screenshots/bullpen-fatigue.png)

Scroll to the boxscore on the same [game page](/game/823921).

## Onboarding tips

First-time visitors get dismissible tips: how to **install the site like an app**, how to **favorite a team** in three steps, and what favoriting unlocks on the Teams page. X them away once you've got it.

![Install this like an app onboarding tip card](/screenshots/onboarding-hint.png)

For the longer walkthrough, see the [getting started guide](/blog/2026-04-01-getting-started-guide).
