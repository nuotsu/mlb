Welcome. If you just landed here, this is the short version of what [MLB.TheOhtani.com](/) is and how to get the most out of it in the first two minutes.

## What this app is

**Mitchell's Live scoreBug** — a free, no-account baseball companion for live scores, standings, stats, and player pages. Think scoreboard energy, not a sports portal stuffed with promos.

Here's what you'll find:

- **[Schedule](/schedule/day)** — today's games with live scorebugs that update as the inning unfolds
- **[Standings](/standings)** — division and league tables for the current season
- **[Stats](/stats)** — leaderboards you can dig into without leaving the page
- **[Teams](/teams)** and **[Players](/player)** — pages for every club and anyone you search for, with year-by-year numbers and hot/cold zones
- **[Spoiler prevention](/teams)** — hide a team's scores until you're ready (handy when you're catching up later)
- **[Stats API playground](/api/v1)** — for the curious, a way to poke at MLB's public stats API yourself

No sign-up. Favorites and preferences stay on your device.

## First thing to do: favorite a team

Favoriting is the one setup step that makes the app feel like _yours_.

1. Open **[Teams](/teams)**.
2. Tap the **star** next to your team.
3. Head back to the **[home page](/)** — their schedule calendar shows up there.

That star also:

- Pins their games to the top of the day and week schedules
- Adds a quick link in the sidebar
- Highlights favorited players in boxscores

You can favorite players and individual games the same way. Stars live in the page header or next to the name — tap once to add, tap again to remove.

## Install it like an app (no App Store)

This site can sit on your home screen or dock like a normal app. Same site, full screen, one tap away.

**On iPhone / iPad (Safari):**

1. Tap the Share button.
2. Scroll and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Tap **Add**.

**On Android / Chrome / Edge:**

- Tap **Install app** if you see the tip on the home page, or
- Open the browser menu and choose **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.

You don't need the App Store or Play Store. It's just a shortcut that opens this site without the browser chrome.

## A quick tour of the sidebar

The menu on the left (or the sidebar button on mobile) is the map:

- **Schedule** — day view for live games, week view for the calendar
- **League** — standings, stats leaders, transactions
- **Browse** — teams and player search
- **Favorites** — whatever you've starred, always one click away
- **Spoiler Prevention** — teams whose scores you want hidden
- **Compare** — put two players side by side

There's also a light/dark toggle at the bottom of the sidebar.
