A few game-page and player-page polish items that landed right after the break. Mostly the kind of live-game detail you want without hunting through the play-by-play.

## ABS challenge dots

Live and final games with ABS now show **remaining challenge dots** to the left of each team score. Remaining challenges light up in T-Mobile magenta — ABS challenges are brought to you by them — and spent ones go dim. The active batting team's indicator stays on the right of the bar so the dots don't shove anything around.

![ABS challenge dots beside Marlins and Astros scores](/screenshots/abs-challenge-dots.png)

See it on any ABS game, e.g. [Marlins @ Astros](/game/824166).

## Homeruns

Game pages now have a **Homeruns** block next to Top Performers and Decisions: batter, season ordinal, RBI label, distance / exit velocity / launch angle, and direction, with the inning on the right.

![Homeruns block from Athletics at Diamondbacks with six homers](/screenshots/homeruns.png)

## Reviews

Same row of game summary blocks now includes **Reviews**: every ABS and traditional play challenge in the game, with outcome coloring for whether the challenging side kept or spent the challenge, plus count and inning.

![Reviews list of ABS challenges with stands and overturned outcomes](/screenshots/reviews.png)

## Player bio

Player pages now surface **H/W** and **Bats / Throws** in the bio list, alongside position, jersey, and the rest of the vitals.

![Player bio with H/W and Bats / Throws on Ohtani](/screenshots/player-bio.png)

See it on any [player page](/player). [Ohtani](/player/660271) is a good example.
